# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want participation in this project to be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent while discussing technical disagreements.
- Give actionable feedback about code, documentation, tests, and design decisions.
- Accept constructive criticism and focus discussion on the work rather than individuals.
- Respect privacy and do not publish credentials, personal data, private logs, or security-sensitive information.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate publication of private or sensitive information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, close, or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary. For sensitive enforcement concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not include vulnerability details in public issues.

This code of conduct applies to project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, and project-related communications.
